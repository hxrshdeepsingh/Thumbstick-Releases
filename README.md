# Thumbstick

**Turn your phone into a game controller for your PC.**

Thumbstick lets you control PC games using your Android phone or iPhone. Connect your phone to your Windows PC over Wi-Fi and use your phone as a wireless gamepad.

**Website:** https://thumbstick.vercel.app/

## How It Works

1. Open Thumbstick on your Windows PC.
2. Open Thumbstick on your phone.
3. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
4. Scan the QR code displayed on your PC using your phone.
5. Once connected, your phone becomes a game controller!

## Windows Driver

Thumbstick may require a separate Windows driver to enable controller functionality.

Download and install the driver separately if required by your Windows application. Find the latest driver in [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hxrshdeepsingh/thumbstick-releases/releases).

## Start Playing

1. Install the Windows application.
2. Install the required Windows driver.
3. Install Thumbstick on your phone.
4. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
5. Scan the QR code to pair your devices.
6. Use your phone's controls to play your favourite PC games!
